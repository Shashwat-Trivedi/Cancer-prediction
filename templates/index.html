How The Prediction System Works

A seamless three-stage machine learning inference pipeline designed for clinical clarity and high predictive fidelity.

01

Biomarker Feature Extraction

Fine Needle Aspirate (FNA) slide images of breast mass cell nuclei are digitized to record 30 geometric dimensions (radius, texture, perimeter, area, concavity).

02

StandardScaler Normalization

Features have wildly differing scales (e.g. area > 2000 vs fractal dimension < 0.1). Preprocessing standardizes each feature using saved training z-scores.

03

Random Forest Inference

An ensemble of decision trees votes on the class outcome (Benign vs. Malignant) and outputs an exact probability confidence percentage.
